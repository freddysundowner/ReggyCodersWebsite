### 🌟 Featured Products
Add a section on the homepage showcasing these major products:

1. **Pointify POS**
   - A widely used point of sale system available as a mobile app, web app, and desktop app.

2. **Tokshop App**
   - A mobile app for live shopping, live auctions, and seamless buying & selling of products.

3. **Banky**
   - A SACCO and microfinance management software that handles loans, member registration, finance, HR, and more.

4. **Shambakit**
   - An AI-driven innovation for farmers. It detects plant diseases, recommends pesticides, shows where to buy them, and provides a public map with markers indicating disease detection locations.

5. **Listing App**
   - A real estate platform for managing and marketing properties, listing them with 3D house viewing capabilities.
